About

The short version

BloomDay is a gentle, ADHD-friendly productivity app that brings task management, focus support, a decorated journal and a growing garden into one daily ritual. Finished tasks earn seeds, seeds buy plants and decor, and daily watering helps the garden bloom, so progress becomes something you can see and come back to.

With more than 200 plant species, 100+ decorations, 19 themed spaces, 18 ambient soundscapes and over 250 clay journal stickers, BloomDay turns everyday planning into a softer, more personal experience. It syncs both ways with Notion, accepts tasks by voice, and is fully available in English, Turkish and Spanish.

Key Features

What makes BloomDay different

200+ Plant Species

Illustrated plants that grow through several stages, from seed to full bloom, each with real botanical trivia in three languages.

19 Garden Themes

Interiors, outdoor gardens and allotments across three environments, from Cosy Cottage to Night Garden and Tuscan Vineyard.

Focus Mode with Ambient Sound

Sessions from 25 to 90 minutes with 18 soundscapes, including lofi, rain, tabla, downbeat, cumbia, afrobeat, birds and a purring cat.

Streaks and Habits

BloomDay Journal

Founder

The story behind BloomDay

EA

Ezgi Acar

Solo Founder

BloomDay was built entirely by Ezgi Acar, a former humanitarian worker who moved into app development during a career change. Using AI tools to move faster, she built BloomDay around a simple idea: productivity tools should feel kind, especially for people whose attention works differently.

The app is built with React Native (Expo), Supabase for the backend, RevenueCat for subscriptions, and Resend for emails. Every plant illustration was designed to bring warmth and personality to the experience.
