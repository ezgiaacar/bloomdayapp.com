Frequently asked questions
Tasks & Seeds
Each completed task gives you 2 seeds, with +1 if you finish inside its time window and another +1 during Focus Mode. A finished focus session adds +3, the daily challenge adds +3 to +10, each achievement adds +5 and a plant reaching full bloom adds +20.
Finish at least one task a day to keep your streak. If you miss a day it resets, and if you have a resilience token BloomDay offers to restore it. Tokens come at the 7, 14, 30 and 100-day streaks and whenever a plant reaches full bloom.
Each morning one challenge appears at the top of your task list, such as finishing 2, 3 or 5 tasks, finishing a task before noon, watering a plant or clearing every task of the day. When you meet it, the card turns green and the seeds arrive at once.
Yes. When adding a task, choose Daily, Weekdays, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, or pick exact dates on a calendar. Each day tracks its own completion. Swipe left on a recurring task and tap Stop to turn it back into a one-time task.
Deleted tasks cannot be recovered. Use the postpone feature instead of deleting tasks you might want to return to later. Swipe left on a task and tap Later to push it to a future date.
Yes. Tap the microphone in Add Task or in the journal and speak. BloomDay uses your phone’s built-in speech recognition and picks up dates and times from what you say, in English, Turkish and Spanish. Voice input is free for everyone.
Open Settings, find Notion under Integrations, sign in and choose one task database. Its tasks appear in BloomDay, and when you complete one in BloomDay it is checked off in Notion too. You can disconnect at any time from the same screen.
Plants & Garden
Plants grow through watering. Tasks earn the seeds that buy plants, and watering makes them grow. Each species grows at its own pace. When a plant reaches full bloom you earn +20 seeds and a resilience token.
Your plant shows how recently it was watered: Thriving (today), Happy (1 day missed), Thirsty (2 days), Wilting (3 days) and Critical (4 or more days). Plants never die. A critical plant recovers as soon as you water it.
More than 200 illustrated species across three environments: Interior (houseplants), Garden (flowers and trees) and Allotment (vegetables and herbs). Free accounts start with the starter species. Plans and Collections add premium cultivars, rare specimens, dwarf fruit trees and heritage vegetables.
Focus & Features
A full-screen timed session. Choose 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes and one of 18 ambient sounds. A breathing plant keeps you company. Finishing the session earns +3 seeds, and BloomDay asks before letting you leave early.
Write a letter to your future self and seal it to a plant. It stays locked until the plant reaches full bloom, then opens on its own. Free accounts can keep up to 3 capsules, and plans make them unlimited.
A notebook for your days. Start from a blank page or a daily, weekly, monthly or habit template, write, then decorate the page with clay stickers, washi tape, stamps you earn and your mood mascot of the day. Pages can be exported as a PDF. Free accounts keep 3 pages and use 15 stickers.
A one-time purchase that unlocks every journal sticker for good, whatever your plan. Ready-made template sets stay with the Bloom, Forest and Eden plans. Eden and lifetime owners already have the kit.
A vision board made of clay pins. Arrange goals, places and small comforts on the board and share it as an image. Free accounts get a starter set of pins, and the board can also sit on your home screen as a widget.
Only you. The mascot you pick stays on your phone. It is never sent to analytics, never backed up and never synced, and it goes back to neutral the next day.
There are 58 badges: 48 achievements across tasks, streaks, garden, watering, seeds and special moments, plus 10 plant collections. Each achievement adds +5 seeds when it unlocks, and a completed collection adds seeds, a badge and a decor piece. See them all under Achievements.
The rarest thing in BloomDay, and money cannot buy them. You earn one at the 7, 14, 30 and 100-day streaks and each time a plant reaches full bloom, and you can hold up to 10. A token restores a broken streak.
Account & Billing
Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose BloomDay and tap Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Your plan stays active until the end of the current period.
Yes. Eden is also sold as a one-time lifetime purchase. Themed Collections, the All Collections bundle and the Journal Kit are one-time purchases too. If you change phones, tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen.
Your data lives on your phone, and while you are signed in BloomDay also keeps a private backup copy tied to your account. If you reinstall and sign in with the same account, your garden and progress come back. Your mood choices are never part of that backup.
Go to Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. This deletes your cloud backup, removes your account and erases the data on this phone. It cannot be undone. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.
Your phone is the main copy. While you are signed in, BloomDay backs it up to your account, so a new phone signed in to the same account gets your garden and progress back. It is a backup for moving phones, not live syncing between two devices used at the same time.
BloomDay is fully available in English, Turkish and Spanish, including plant names, botanical trivia, achievements, the journal and notifications. You can switch languages anytime from Settings.
