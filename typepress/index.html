Section I

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

What We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No accounts, no names, no tracking. Everything you create stays on your device.

How We Use It

We do not use data for analytics, ads, or profiling.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, your pages are stored in your own iCloud Drive and kept up to date across your devices. They never pass through our servers, and we cannot see them. Sync stays off until you turn it on.

Purchases

TypePress Pro is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app identifier and your purchase record, never your name, your email, or your writing.

Your Rights

You can contact us anytime with questions or concerns.

Security

No system is 100% secure, but we keep things simple and local.
