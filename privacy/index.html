Privacy Policy

01 — Overview

Simple by design

BloomDay is a task planner and virtual garden app. We collect only what is necessary to run the app and understand how it is used. We do not sell data, build behavioural profiles, or share your information with advertisers beyond what is required to serve ads on the free plan.

We believe privacy should be the default, not a setting you have to find. This policy is written to be read, not to obscure. If anything is unclear, write to us.

02 — Data We Collect

What we know about you

@

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password, or the identifier Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple. This is used to authenticate you and to keep your backup tied to your account. We do not collect your phone number or any other personal identifier.

☘

App data and backup

Your tasks, garden, plants, streaks, achievements, journal pages, time capsules and preferences live on your device. While you are signed in, BloomDay also keeps a private backup copy of this data on our servers, tied to your account, so you can get your garden back on a new phone. Only your account can read it. Your daily mood mascot is never part of this backup.

☼

Usage analytics

To improve the app, we collect anonymous usage patterns such as which screens you visit, which features you use, session duration, and general device information (model, operating system, app version). This data is not linked to your email or personal identity, and it never includes your mood. You can turn it off in Settings → Help → Privacy → Share Usage Data.

♪

Microphone and calendar, only when you ask

When you tap the microphone to add a task or journal text, your phone's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. Where your phone supports it, this happens on the device. Otherwise Apple or Google processes the audio under their own terms. BloomDay does not record or store your voice. If you export tasks to your calendar, BloomDay asks for calendar access and only writes the tasks you export.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or photos. Your daily mood stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere. The only device identifier that may be used for advertising is your advertising ID, and only if you explicitly grant permission through the iOS tracking prompt (see Advertising below).
03 — How We Use It

Only what runs the app

⚿

Authentication

Your email and credentials are used to create and protect your account via Supabase. We support email/password and Apple Sign In.

↻

Storage and backup

Your data is saved on your device so your garden persists between sessions. While you are signed in, a backup copy is sent to your account every few minutes and restored only when a device has nothing saved, for example after a reinstall or on a new phone.

⚙

Notifications

If you enable task reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notifications are never routed through a server. Daily and weekly recurring reminders fire locally.

✉

Account communications

We may use your email for critical account events only: password reset and account deletion confirmation. We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional content.

04 — Analytics

How we understand usage

BloomDay uses PostHog, a third-party product analytics service, to understand how users interact with the app so we can improve it. PostHog is used in a privacy-first mode with no behavioural profiling or cross-site tracking.

What PostHog receives

Screen views, feature interactions (e.g. "completed a task", "opened garden"), session duration, app version, device model, operating system version, and a randomly generated anonymous ID. PostHog does not receive your email, name, tasks, journal entries, or any content you create in the app.

Your control

Analytics are enabled by default to help us improve the app. You can turn them off anytime in Settings → Help → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When disabled, PostHog stops collecting data immediately, and no events are sent from your device.

Storage and retention

PostHog stores aggregated usage data on its EU-hosted servers (GDPR-compliant, ISO 27001 certified). Event data is retained for 12 months, after which it is automatically deleted. We do not export this data or combine it with any other source.

Learn more about PostHog's privacy practices at posthog.com/privacy.

05 — Advertising

Ads on the free plan

The free plan includes occasional ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear at most once per day and are never shown during Focus Mode or during onboarding.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, BloomDay asks for your permission before any tracking occurs. You can choose "Ask App Not to Track" and BloomDay will request non-personalised ads only — no behavioural profile is built on you. If you choose "Allow", Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. Either choice is fully respected.

Your control

You can change this permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → BloomDay.

Upgrading to any paid plan (Bloom, Forest, or Eden) removes all ads permanently.

06 — Third Parties

Who else touches your data

Supabase

Account authentication and your private app backup. Stores your email, hashed password, the backup copy of your app data and, if you connect Notion, your Notion connection. Each account can only reach its own rows.

GDPR compliant · SOC 2

PostHog

Anonymous product analytics to help us improve the app. EU-hosted, no behavioural profiling. Opt-out available in Settings.

EU-hosted · Opt-out available

RevenueCat

Subscription management via Apple App Store and Google Play. Receives a transaction token only.

No payment data shared

Google AdMob

Ads on the free plan. With your consent (via iOS App Tracking Transparency), uses your advertising identifier to show relevant ads. Without consent, shows non-personalised ads only.

Free plan · Consent-based

Notion

Only if you connect it. BloomDay stores a Notion access token and the name and ID of the database you choose, reads tasks from that database and marks them done when you complete them in BloomDay. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Optional · You choose the database

Resend

If the app runs into an error, a short technical report (the error message, app screen and device model) is emailed to the developer through Resend so the bug can be fixed. It does not include your tasks, journal or email address.

Error reports only
No other third parties have access to your data. We do not use Facebook SDK, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Firebase Analytics, or any other tracking service beyond what is listed above.
07 — Storage & Retention

Where your data lives

●

On your device, backed up to your account

Your tasks, plants, streaks, achievements, journal pages and garden live on your device, with a private backup tied to your account while you are signed in. Bloom Board pins, journal folders and your mood choice stay on the device only.

●

Deleting the app

Removing the app deletes the data on that device. Your backup stays with your account, so signing in again on a new install brings your garden back. To remove the backup as well, delete your account.

●

Account deletion

Settings → Delete Account deletes your cloud backup, removes your account and erases the data on this device. If you connected Notion, disconnect it first or email us and we will remove the stored connection.

08 — Your Rights

What you can ask of us

Under GDPR (EU), KVKK (Turkey), CCPA (California), and similar regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

1

Access

Ask us what personal data we hold. On our servers that means your email address, your app backup and, if you connected it, your Notion connection.

2

Deletion

Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing us. This removes your account and your backup. Data on your device is erased at the same time.

3

Export and copies

Export your tasks to your phone's calendar from Settings → Export Data, and export journal pages as a PDF. For a copy of your full backup, email us and we will send it.

4

Opt-out of analytics

Turn off analytics anytime in Settings → Help → Privacy → Share Usage Data. This takes effect immediately.

09 — Children

Not for children under 13

BloomDay is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
