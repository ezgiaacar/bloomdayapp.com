01 · Tasks

The root of everything

Every seed you earn, every plant you buy and every streak you build starts with finishing a task.

1

Add a task

Type it in, or tap the microphone and say it. BloomDay listens with your phone’s own speech recognition and picks up dates and times from the sentence, in English, Turkish and Spanish. Give it a category: Work, Health, Home, Learn, Social or Self.

2

Set a time window

Give the task a start time and a duration. Finish it inside that window and you earn a bonus seed.

3

Add a reminder

Choose how many minutes before the task the notification should arrive.

4

Add a note

Attach a short note for context. It appears in italics under the task title.

5

Complete it

Tap the circle to finish the task and earn 2 seeds. Swipe left to postpone or delete.

The calendar scrolls 180 days back and 365 days ahead. Tap any day to see its tasks or plan ahead. Finish every task of the day and BloomDay marks the moment with a short closing note. If you add fifteen or more tasks in one day, a mascot checks in to ask how you are feeling.

05 · Focus Mode

A quiet room for deep work

A full-screen timer with ambient sound, a breathing plant and a small reward at the end.

Choose 25, 45, 60 or 90 minutes. A breathing plant animation keeps you grounded.

Pick one of 18 ambient sounds: lofi, rain, birds, piano, tabla, downbeat, cumbia, afrobeat, vinyl, a purring cat and more.

Tasks you finish during a session earn a bonus seed, and a completed session earns +3. Ending early skips the reward.

If you try to leave, BloomDay asks first. Cancel takes you back to the timer.

09 · Journal New

Pages you decorate yourself

Your garden has a notebook. Write about your day on a blank page or a template, then decorate it with clay stickers, washi tape and marker text.

1

Start a page

Pick a blank page or a ready-made daily, weekly, monthly or habit template. Tap the question of the day to open a page with that question as its title.

2

Write

Add titles, notes and stamp-style text. Speak instead of typing if you like. Link the page to a plant and choose your paper.

3

Decorate

More than 250 clay stickers in 18 sets: washi tapes, day and month headers, dates, numbers, speech bubbles, weather, critters, treats, tabs, habits and doodles. Stamps and badges unlock as you grow, and today’s mood mascot is always there to stick on the page.

4

Keep and share

Saved pages open again in the same editor for changes. Pin favourites to your Bloom Board, or export pages as a PDF.

Free accounts can keep 3 journal pages and use 15 stickers. Bloom, Forest and Eden open more sticker sets and unlimited pages. The Journal Kit is a one-time purchase that unlocks every sticker for good. Ready-made template sets stay with the plans.

14 · Notion & Recurring Tasks New

Bring your own system

BloomDay connects to Notion and Obsidian. With Notion, choose one task database and your Notion tasks appear in BloomDay. Finish one here and it is checked off in Notion too, while your garden grows from it.

1

Connect Notion

Open Settings, find Notion under Integrations, sign in and choose the database to sync.

2

Work in either place

Completions flow both ways. You can disconnect at any time from the same screen.

3

Set a repeat

Once, Daily, Weekdays, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, or exact dates you pick on a calendar. Each day tracks its own completion.

4

Stop repeating

Swipe left on a recurring task and tap Stop. It becomes a one-time task and its reminder is cancelled.

15 · Obsidian New

Your vault, in the garden

BloomDay reads the checkboxes in your Obsidian vault and turns them into tasks. Nothing to set up online, and your notes stay where they are.

1

Choose your vault

Open Settings, find Obsidian under Integrations and tap to choose your vault folder. If your vault lives in iCloud Drive, look inside the Obsidian folder there.

2

Write tasks as usual

Any line written as - [ ] task in your notes shows up in today’s list.

3

Finish it anywhere

Complete the task in BloomDay and the box is ticked in your note too, while your garden grows from it.

4

New tasks find a home

Tasks you add in BloomDay are written to a note called BloomDay at the top of your vault.

16 · Add Tasks by Email & Share New

Send it to your garden

Tasks do not have to start in the app. Email them, share them from another app, or save a page from your browser, and they land in BloomDay.

1

Find your address

Open Settings, then Integrations, then Add tasks by email. You get a personal address ending in @bloomdayapp.com. Keep it to yourself, since anything sent to it becomes a task.

2

Write or forward a mail

The subject becomes the task and the first line of the mail becomes its note. Write a day or a time in the subject, such as “Call the dentist tomorrow at 10”, and BloomDay sets it for you, in English, Turkish or Spanish.

3

Share from any app

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, tap Share and choose BloomDay. A shared link becomes a task starting with “Check:”, and the link is kept as its note. On Mac, turn it on once in System Settings, under Extensions, then Sharing.

4

Save from Safari

Turn on the BloomDay extension in Safari, then tap its icon on any page. Edit the task and the note, and tap Send to my garden. A Chrome extension is on its way.

Tasks sent while the app is closed are waiting for you the next time you open BloomDay.

20 · Tips

Getting the most from your garden

Practical advice for building habits and growing your garden at a pace you can keep.

Water every day

Growth comes from watering. Open the app, water a plant, and let the rest follow.

Use Focus Mode for the hard ones

Tasks finished during a session earn a bonus seed, and the session itself adds three more.

Save for rare plants

Forest and Eden plants cost hundreds of seeds. Saving for them should feel earned.

Guard your streak

One finished task keeps a streak alive. On slow days, do the smallest one.

Speak your tasks

Say “call mum tomorrow at 6” and BloomDay fills in the day and the time for you. It works from your watch too.

Close the day on paper

A short journal page at night is enough. A sticker and three lines still count.

Every reward should be a consequence of real work, not a shortcut around it. Growth is quiet. It happens one task, one day, one leaf at a time.
